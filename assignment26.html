Box Sizing: content-box vs. border-box

Why box-sizing: border-box is universally preferred in modern responsive development:

  • content-box (default): Total Width = width + padding-left + padding-right + border-left + border-right. Spacings expand the element beyond its defined width!
  • border-box (recommended): Total Width = specified width. Padding and borders are absorbed inside the box, guaranteeing predictable layouts.
