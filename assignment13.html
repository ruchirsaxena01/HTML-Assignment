Academic Terminology (Description List)

Continuous Assessment (CA)
Internal evaluation comprising mid-term examinations, assignments, and attendance (30 marks for theory).
End Semester Examination (EE)
University-wide theoretical or practical examination held at the conclusion of each semester (70 marks for theory).
Total Credits
The academic weighting assigned to lectures, tutorials, and practical hours (Semester I total: 22 credits).
Artificial Intelligence Specialization
Integrated curriculum covering Python, Data Analytics, Machine Learning, and Neural Networks across 6 semesters.
