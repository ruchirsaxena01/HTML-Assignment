Position & Stacking Context Explanation

This layout demonstrates four positioning techniques:

  • position: sticky on the top navigation bar keeps it visible while scrolling.
  • position: relative on the hero element establishes a containing block for its children.
  • position: absolute with z-index: 1 creates the dark semi-transparent tint overlay.
  • z-index: 2 lifts the headline text above the overlay for crisp readability.
  • position: absolute with z-index: 3 pins the floating student badge to the bottom-right corner.
