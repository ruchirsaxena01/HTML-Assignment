Design Choices & Rationale

Color Palette: Deep University Navy (#1a365d) conveys academic focus and stability, complemented by Teal (#319795) for prominent calls to action.

Typography: Modern system font stack ensures zero web font download overhead and immediate readability.

Layout: Max-width container of 820px maintains comfortable line length across laptop and desktop screens.

Maintainability: Theme variables are centralized in :root, enabling rapid sitewide customization.
